Content notes

About this foundation

Curriculum author Swar Music and More

The learning path follows the shared foundation found across Tamil and Karnataka Carnatic teaching, while keeping room for a teacher's preferred order and pathāntaram.

  • The foundation contains 14 Sarali, 9 core Janta plus one published Vakra Janta sequence, 5 Melsthayi, 2 core Dhatu varisais plus one extended sequence, 5 Keezh / Mandra-sthayi varisais and the 7 canonical sapta-tāḷa alankarams.
  • Tamil Nadu Grade 1 specifies Sarali, Janta and Melsthayi in three speeds.
  • Grade 2 introduces Dhattu, Keezh-sthayi and alankarams before the composition stage; this app intentionally stops there.
  • Māyāmāḷavagowla and Ādi tāḷam are the initial defaults, not permanent restrictions.
  • The playable sequences follow the published karnATik beginner notation; Tamil presentation and audio are derived from the same swara records. Commas are continuation beats: Sustain holds one sound across them, while Sustain off releases early into silence.
  • A teacher's pathāntaram remains authoritative where a sequence or pronunciation differs.

A calm foundation

Using SWAR meaningfully

01

Starting with SWAR

Choose a comfortable śruti with a teacher or adult. Listen to one complete cycle, then join gently. Stay in first speed until pitch, breath and tāḷa feel steady; move to second and third speed only without strain.

02

Learning with a teacher

Use the canvas as a shared visual and sing with the teacher’s pronunciation and pathāntaram. Let the teacher decide śruti, tempo, rāgam and when to progress. The app is best for recall between lessons.

03

Teaching with SWAR

Demonstrate first, then let the learner echo while keeping hand tāḷa. Assign a short named lesson and settings, not an open-ended session. In online lessons, take turns instead of trying to sing simultaneously through network delay.

What the synthesiser can do

It can give a repeatable pitch path, pulse and sustained-note model. It cannot judge voice quality, breathing, gamaka, pronunciation, strain or a school’s pathāntaram; a human teacher remains the authority.
