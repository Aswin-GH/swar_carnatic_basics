Tāḷa metronome

7 tāḷas × 5 jātis · 35 combinations

Keep the hand action—not only the sound. The 35 combinations come from the seven sūḷādi tāḷas crossed with five laghu jātis.

1Clap
LaghuOne clap followed by finger counts; its length changes with jāti.
DrutamTwo akṣaras: clap, then wave.
AnudrutamOne akṣara: a single clap.

Structure checked against the CBSE Carnatic syllabus and the app’s researched 35-tāḷa dataset.
